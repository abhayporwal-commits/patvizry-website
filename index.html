Who we are

Built on
precision.

Patvizry Services is a patent and trademark consulting practice led by a team of Registered Indian Patent Agents and Trademark Agents with over 25 years of experience in intellectual property.

We work with inventors, startups, and R&D teams who need someone who understands both the legal framework and the technical substance. Our evaluations go beyond patentability checklists - we assess commercial viability, claim strength, and prior art landscapes.

We have filed across jurisdictions and technology domains. Our network of foreign associates handles coordinated prosecution in the US, Europe, and Asia, managed by us to keep quality consistent.

What guides us

  • Precision in claim drafting
  • Honest patentability assessments (we will tell you if it is not worth filing)
  • Long-term IP strategy, not just one-off applications
